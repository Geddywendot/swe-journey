## Lessons (5‑8 official resources)

1. **FastAPI – Background Tasks** – `fastapi.tiangolo.com/advanced/background-tasks/` – 30 min: Learn how to schedule work after a response and ensure cleanup.
2. **FastAPI – Deployment** – `fastapi.tiangolo.com/deployment/` – 45 min: Guidance on containerising FastAPI, using gunicorn, and handling graceful shutdowns.
3. **SQLAlchemy – Performance Tips** – `docs.sqlalchemy.org/en/20/faq/performance.html` – 30 min: Detect N+1 queries, use eager loading, and optimise session usage.
4. **PostgreSQL – EXPLAIN** – `www.postgresql.org/docs/current/sql-explain.html` – 25 min: Interpret execution plans, understand cost, and identify missing indexes.
5. **Locust – Load Testing** – `locust.io/` – 40 min: Write a repeatable script to simulate traffic, collect latency data, and compare runs.
6. **Docker – Production Images** – `docs.docker.com/engine/reference/builder/` – 20 min: Build a minimal image for FastAPI with `uvicorn` and multi‑stage builds.
7. **GitHub Actions – CI** – `docs.github.com/en/actions` – 30 min: Set up a CI workflow that runs performance tests on each push.
