# ADR Template

**Title:** <Short descriptive title>

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

---

## Context

Explain the background, problem, and why a decision is needed. Include relevant constraints, stakeholders, and any prior attempts.

## Decision

State the chosen solution clearly and concisely. Mention who made the decision and any consensus reached.

## Alternatives considered

List alternative approaches that were evaluated, with brief pros and cons for each. Explain why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Describe the implications of the decision, both positive and negative. Include any follow‑up actions, migration steps, or deprecation plans.

---

*Write an ADR whenever a significant architectural, tooling, or process choice is made that will affect future development or maintenance.*
