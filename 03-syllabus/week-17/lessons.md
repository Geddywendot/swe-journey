## Lessons

1. **System Design Fundamentals** – Capacity planning, scaling, and fault tolerance basics. *Time: 45 min* – https://martinfowler.com/articles/architecture-design.html
2. **Designing for Scale with FastAPI** – Strategies for horizontal scaling, sharding, and async processing. *Time: 45 min* – https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/performance/
3. **Express.js Architecture Guide** – Core concepts of Express middleware, routing, and scaling. *Time: 45 min* – https://expressjs.com/en/guide/routing.html
4. **MongoDB Data Modeling** – Schema design, indexing, and aggregation pipelines. *Time: 45 min* – https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/core/data-modeling-introduction/
5. **PostgreSQL vs MongoDB Comparison** – Trade‑offs in consistency, query capabilities, and operational overhead. *Time: 45 min* – https://www.citusdata.com/blog/2020/04/08/postgresql-vs-mongodb/
6. **Creating System Design Diagrams** – Using draw.io or Mermaid to visualize components and data flow. *Time: 45 min* – https://mermaid-js.github.io/mermaid/#/n00b/flowchart
7. **Presentation Best Practices** – Designing a concise slide deck for architectural reviews. *Time: 30 min* – https://slidedeck.io/blog/presentations-best-practices
