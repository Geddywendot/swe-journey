## Concepts

1. **Scoping a Feature** – Define the boundaries of what the feature will do, its user stories, and acceptance criteria before coding. *Explain it back.*
2. **Writing a README for a Stranger** – Include clear sections: overview, setup, running locally, testing, Docker build, deployment steps, and contribution guidelines. *Explain it back.*
3. **Architecture Decision Record (ADR)** – A short document that records a significant architectural choice, why it was made, alternatives considered, and consequences. *Explain it back.*
4. **Demo vs. Deployment** – A demo is a temporary, often local showcase; a deployment is a stable, publicly accessible service with monitoring and versioning. *Explain it back.*
5. **Partner Pull‑Request Review** – Provide constructive feedback, check tests, documentation, and adherence to the project’s contribution standards. *Explain it back.*
