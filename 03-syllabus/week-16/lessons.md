## Lessons

1. **fastapi-cache2 Documentation** – Guide to adding response caching in FastAPI. *Time: 45 min* – https://github.com/long2ice/fastapi-cache
2. **Redis Official Docs** – Using Redis as a cache and message broker. *Time: 45 min* – https://redis.io/docs/
3. **RQ (Redis Queue) Guide** – Simple background job queue with Redis. *Time: 40 min* – https://python-rq.org/
4. **slowapi Rate Limiting** – Rate limiting middleware for FastAPI. *Time: 30 min* – https://github.com/laurentS/slowapi
5. **React Query Caching** – Client‑side data caching and stale‑while‑revalidate. *Time: 45 min* – https://tanstack.com/query/v4/docs/react/overview
6. **Axios Interceptors for Caching** – Adding a cache layer to Axios requests. *Time: 30 min* – https://axios-http.com/docs/interceptors
7. **Docker Compose with Redis** – Adding a Redis service to the stack. *Time: 30 min* – https://docs.docker.com/compose/redis/
8. **Prometheus Rate‑Limit Metrics** – Exporting rate‑limit metrics. *Time: 30 min* – https://prometheus.io/docs/practices/rate_limiting/
