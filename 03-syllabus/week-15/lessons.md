## Lessons

1. **FastAPI Structured Logging** – Configuring `loguru` or Python `logging` for JSON output. *Time: 45 min* – https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/advanced/custom-request-and-route/
2. **Health Check Endpoint** – Implementing a lightweight `/health` route returning status. *Time: 30 min* – https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/first-steps/#path-operations
3. **Prometheus Client for FastAPI** – Adding `prometheus_client` to expose metrics. *Time: 45 min* – https://github.com/prometheus/client_python
4. **Docker Healthchecks** – Defining `HEALTHCHECK` in Dockerfile for API container. *Time: 30 min* – https://docs.docker.com/engine/reference/builder/#healthcheck
5. **Basic Monitoring with Grafana** – Setting up a simple Grafana dashboard to visualize Prometheus metrics. *Time: 45 min* – https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/getting-started/getting-started-prometheus/
6. **Log Aggregation with Loki** – Sending JSON logs to Loki for search and analysis. *Time: 45 min* – https://grafana.com/docs/loki/latest/clients/python/
7. **Alerting Basics** – Configuring a simple alert rule for health‑check failures. *Time: 30 min* – https://prometheus.io/docs/alerting/latest/overview/
